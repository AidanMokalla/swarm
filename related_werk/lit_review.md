# Background work

Research in different types of trait/behavior contagion in multi-agent systems has typically followed followed four dimensions:

1) How the trait is induced in the source model
2) What transmission channel is being measured
3) What and how the receiver of a trait is measured
4) What topology or topologies the study uses

## How the trait is induced

There are three well-understood ways to induce a behavior in a model: through prompting, through fine-tuning of the model, or through activation steering. 

**Prompting** 

[Misalignment Contagion](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02751) finds that LMs become more anti-social after social-dilemma games, and more so when their partners are steered to be malicious. The steering is prompt-based and the mitigation is black-box.
- [LLM Agents in Interaction](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.02896) finds linguistic and personality alignment between persona-prompted agents, and the alignment is asymmetric. It's behavioral only.
